About GWM & Jurkon Studio

GWM (Go With Me) is an information portal for concerts, festivals, hiking, and finding travel companions across Indonesian cities. The site is built so event information is easy to find, share, and connect with local communities.

GWM does not sell tickets and does not produce events. We curate public information and provide community features (invites, city forums, messaging) so people can connect more safely and orderly.

Partnership model (revenue)

Once the production domain is live, GWM offers transparent partnership paths:

  • Featured placements — priority slots on the home page and event lists, clearly labelled.
  • Sponsorships & partners — promotion packages for organizers, brands, or open-trip operators.
  • Ticket affiliates — official ticket links with partner parameters when a contract exists.

A partnership interest form is available on the Help page. There are no hidden fees for end users who only browse info or look for company.
