About GoWithDME

GoWithDME is an information portal for concerts, festivals, hiking trips, and finding travel companions in cities across Indonesia.

It Started with Going Alone

It began when I went to watch Hindia and .Feast perform at the Tau Tau Festival. I went by myself. I didn’t know anyone there, and I felt a little lost standing in the crowd, so for a while I simply watched the show alone.

Then I met two strangers who ended up joining me. We watched the rest of the set together, and the evening turned out very differently from how it started.

That’s when I realized that many people actually want to attend events but end up skipping them because they have no one to go with. GoWithDME was created to solve that problem.

How GoWithDME Works

  1. 1. Find Events
    Search for events, cities, or mountains.
  2. 2. Check Details
    View dates, prices, locations, and other essential information.
  3. 3. Ask Dea / Invite Others
    Ask Dea AI if you have questions, or create an invitation to join others.
  4. 4. Go Together
    Meet new friends, attend events, or hike mountains together. You’ll never have to go alone again.

What GoWithDME Is Not

We would rather be clear about this from the start:

  • GoWithDME does not sell tickets.
  • GoWithDME does not organize events.
  • GoWithDME is not a party to any transactions.

GoWithDME simply curates information and provides community features. Tickets are bought from the official seller or organizer, and any agreement, payment, or arrangement is between you and them.

Three Key Features

  • Events — Curated event information, gathered in one place.
  • Search — Find events by city, category, or date.
  • Dea AI — Your go-to assistant for questions about events.

Collaborations

GoWithDME works with organizers, open trip providers, and ticketing partners in the following ways:

  • Featured / Highlighted — priority placements, clearly labelled
  • Sponsors & partners — promotional packages for event organizers & open trips
  • Ticket affiliates — official links with partner parameters when a contract is in place
