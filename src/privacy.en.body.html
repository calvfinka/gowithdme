Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026. Prepared with reference to Indonesian Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (PDP Law). This English version is provided for convenience; if there is any difference, the Indonesian version prevails.

This text is an initial draft. Have a lawyer review it, and make sure every promise (for example, retention periods) is actually carried out by the system.

1. Data controller

Jurkon Studio, operator of GWM. Contact: halo@gowithdme.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Without an account: your packing checklist and saved events are stored only in your browser (localStorage) and are not sent to our servers.
  • With an account: email, nickname, profile photo (if you sign in with Google), and home city.
  • Meet-up feature: the invite content and the WhatsApp number you enter. The number is visible only to the host and to participants approved by the host.
  • Account security: when you log in, with your permission we may record approximate GPS location, IP address, and device type to detect suspicious access.

3. Purpose & legal basis

To create and secure accounts, display profiles, and keep the community safe. Location data is processed only with your explicit consent and is not shown to other users. We do not sell personal data.

4. Third-party providers

  • Supabase: database, authentication, and poster storage.
  • Google: Google login and Google Fonts (your IP address is sent to Google when fonts load).
  • jsDelivr: delivery of JavaScript libraries.

5. Retention

Account data is kept while the account is active. Login security logs are kept for at most 90 days. After an account is deleted, personal data is deleted or anonymized, except what we are required to keep by law.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, withdraw consent for, and restrict the processing of your personal data. Send a request to the email above; we respond within 3 business days.

7. Security

Data access is protected by row-level security (RLS), and passwords are not stored in readable form. No system is immune; contact us immediately if you suspect your account is being misused.

8. Children

The service is intended for users aged 17 and above. If we find children's data without a guardian's consent, it will be deleted.

9. Changes

Material changes will be announced on this page with an updated date. See also the Terms & Conditions.
