Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. Prepared with reference to Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022) and community-platform best practices.

This is the official GWM policy. For questions or data-subject requests, contact gowithdme@jurkon.my.id.

1. Data controller & owner

Controller: Jurkon Studio (“we”, “GWM”).
Service: Information portal for concerts, festivals, hiking, meet-up invites, and city communities under the GWM (Go With Me) brand.
Contact: gowithdme@jurkon.my.id · WhatsApp 0822 4356 2960.

2. Data we collect

  • Without an account: packing checklist, saved events, short search history, and recently viewed events — stored only in your browser (localStorage), not sent to our servers.
  • With an account: email, nickname, profile photo (if you sign in with Google), home city, bio, interests, and privacy preferences you set.
  • Invites & community: invite content, WhatsApp numbers you enter (visible only to host and approved members), forum posts, and moderation reports.
  • Security: with your permission we may record approximate GPS, IP address, and device type at login to detect suspicious access (limited retention).
  • Anonymous visit stats: time, page, language, device type, and referrer. No IP storage, no third-party tracking cookies, and no link to account identity.
  • Sponsorship leads: organizer name, contact email/WA, and package notes submitted via the official form — used only to follow up on partnership requests.

3. Purposes

  • Provide and improve the service (events, invites, community, messaging).
  • Account security and abuse prevention.
  • Aggregate statistics to understand usage (no individual profiles).
  • Respond to sponsorship requests and content-removal notices.
  • Comply with applicable Indonesian law.

4. Legal basis & retention

Processing is based on contract performance (account & features), legitimate interests (security & anonymous stats), and/or consent (location, public profile). Account data is kept while the account is active; you may request deletion. Security logs and anonymous stats are kept only as long as operationally needed (max. 90 days for detailed login logs unless under investigation).

5. Third parties

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and storage (RLS enabled).
  • Open-Meteo — weather data; requests are made from your browser (your IP may be visible to the provider).
  • Google — OAuth sign-in (if you choose it) and fonts/CDN loaded by the site.
  • Public CDNs (jsDelivr / unpkg) for required libraries.

We do not sell personal data. Partners receive only what is necessary for the services above.

6. Cookies & local storage

We use localStorage/sessionStorage for preferences (theme, language, checklist, saved events) and auth session tokens. There are no third-party advertising cookies. After the production domain is live this policy still applies; if optional analytics cookies are added we will offer a choice and update this text.

7. Your rights

You may access, correct, delete, restrict, and withdraw consent regarding your personal data, and object as provided by law. Contact us at the email above. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

The service is intended for ages 17+ (or with parent/guardian permission). We do not knowingly collect data from younger children.

9. Security

Access is protected by authentication, Row Level Security (RLS), and data minimisation. No system is 100% secure; report suspected breaches promptly.

10. Changes

Material changes are marked by the update date above. Continued use after an update means you understand the latest version. See also Terms & Conditions and Copyright & Ownership.
