How to get there

Location: Kota Lama Semarang (Hotel Kotta Semarang), Semarang.

Tawang and Poncol stations serve trains; Ahmad Yani airport is close to town. Trans Semarang (BRT) and ride-hailing operate in the city.

Check the latest route and travel time in Google Maps. For busy events, ride-hailing is usually easier than driving yourself, though pick-up points tend to be crowded after the show.
