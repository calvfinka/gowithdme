How to get there

Location: Basecamp Selo, Boyolali (titik kumpul Jakarta tersedia), Magelang.

The hike starts from the Gunung Merbabu basecamp. The meeting point and transport from your departure city are arranged by the trip organizer; ask about the gathering time, pick-up location, and whether transport from the meeting point to the basecamp is included in the price.

If you travel on your own, plan to arrive at the basecamp the day before or very early, since mountain roads are slower than map apps estimate and mobile signal may drop.

Tips before you go

  • Check the difficulty and your fitness. Level for this trip: menengah.
  • Bring a warm jacket, rain gear, a headlamp with spare batteries, comfortable hiking shoes, and enough water.
  • Confirm what the price covers (entry permit, transport, meals, guide) before paying.
  • Tell family or friends your route and schedule, and follow your guide and the trail manager's rules.
  • Carry all your trash back down.
  • The summit is around 3,145 m above sea level, noticeably colder up top, so pack layers.
  • This month falls in the rainy season in most of Indonesia; bring a raincoat or compact umbrella and check the forecast the day before.
  • This is a multi-day event: check whether your ticket covers one day or all days, and book accommodation if needed.
