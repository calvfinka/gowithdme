Cowboy Festival comes to Jakarta for two days, 5-6 December 2026, at Stadion Madya, Gelora Bung Karno, with a Desa Cowboy theme. Day 1 (5 Dec): Banda Neira, Lomba Sihir, JKT48 and DNA x MC Parkz. Prices exclude tax and admin fee. Tickets are sold via ADDTIX.ID.
