One day trip (ODT) to Mount Papandayan with Atlantik Traveller Indonesia, starting from Rp180,000/person. Meeting point & price: Jakarta & Bekasi Rp370,000 • Bandung, Ciamis & Tasikmalaya Rp325,000 • Garut area Rp300,000 • Basecamp (no transport) Rp180,000. Trekking dates November 2026: weekends 1, 7, 8, 14, 15, 21, 22, 28, 29; weekdays 3, 12, 16, 26. Trekking dates December 2026: weekends 5, 6, 12, 13, 19, 20, 26, 27; weekdays 1, 10, 17, 24, 25, 31. The dates above are trekking dates; departure is the night before (e.g. trek on the 7th departs the night of the 6th). Itinerary: evening meeting points Jakarta 20:00-21:00, Ciamis 21:00-21:30, Bandung 22:00-23:00, Tasikmalaya 22:00-23:00, Garut 01:00-01:30; arrive at basecamp 02:30-03:30; trek & explore 04:00-13:00 (View Sunrise, Dead Forest, Edelweiss Garden, Pondok Saladah); meal & prep 13:00-14:00; return trip, back in Jakarta around 21:00. INCLUDED: round-trip transport; driver, fuel, toll & parking; entrance ticket; tour guide; 1 meal after trekking; bread snack; mineral water; sticker/pin; standard first aid; basecamp; camera documentation (min. 13 participants). EXCLUDED: personal gear; personal snacks; raincoat; pocket money; personal medicine. Terms: open to everyone (solo, with friends/family/partner); limited seats; booking is valid after a Rp150,000 down payment (full payment by D-3); participants must be physically fit and follow the guide's instructions.
