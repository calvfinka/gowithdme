Why GWM Magazine

Light and simple

One XML file. No plugins and no external JavaScript libraries; the look is built with plain CSS.

Easy to set up with Setup Mode

Open yourblog/#atur, tick and choose options, preview them, then copy the result into the Display Settings gadget.

Free for Blogger

Use it on personal or commercial blogs. One condition: keep the “Template by GWM” credit.

Features

Magazine layout

Post cards, a hero with a search box, topic tiles, and a sidebar, matching the gowithdme.com blog.

Light and dark

Light and dark modes, with theme and ID/EN language buttons for visitors in the hamburger menu.

Clock and date

A clock and date in the header (WIB, the time zone can be changed).

Sticky search

The search box stays attached to the header as you scroll.

Hamburger menu

Topics, quick actions (random, share, copy link, RSS), and info links.

Full article pages

Table of contents, reading time, share buttons, text size, “Read also”, and a progress bar.

Numbered Layout

Layout sections are named and numbered 1 to 13 in Indonesian, so they are easy to find.

Responsive

Comfortable on phones, tablets, and desktops, with no sideways-scrolling pages.

How to install

  1. Download the XML filePress the Download Free button above.
  2. Upload it in BloggerOpen Blogger, choose Theme, click the arrow next to Customize, choose Backup/Restore, then Upload the XML file.
  3. Fill in menus and sidebarOpen Layout and fill in the sections by their numbers.
  4. Tune the lookOpen yourblog/#atur to adjust the look, then copy the result into the Display Settings gadget.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free for commercial blogs?

Yes. The template is free to use on personal and commercial sites, and you may change how it looks.

Can I remove the footer credit?

Not in the free version. The “Template by GWM” text with its link to gowithdme.com must stay. A paid version without the credit will be available.

What kind of blog is it for?

News, opinion, magazine, and long-form blogs that need many topics and a tidy layout.

How do I change colors and the logo?

The template has a built-in text logo and an optional image logo. Other display settings are handled by Setup Mode at yourblog/#atur.

Does it support dark mode and two languages?

Yes. Visitors can switch between light/dark themes and Indonesian/English from the hamburger menu.

When will the WordPress version be available?

There is no date yet. For now this template is for Blogger only. Check back on this page.

What if something goes wrong?

Email the address in the Contact section below with your blog address and a screenshot of the problem.
